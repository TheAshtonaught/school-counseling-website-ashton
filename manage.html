Manage

Manage describes how the school counseling program is organized so that time, priorities, and partnerships remain focused on student success.

What It Means

What Does Manage Mean?

An effective school counseling program does not happen by accident. The Manage component provides the structure needed to turn student needs into an intentional plan.

At Houston High School, the school counselor uses student data, identified needs, program goals, calendars, use-of-time information, collaboration, and stakeholder feedback to determine how school counseling services are organized and delivered.

Managing the program helps ensure that services are equitable, purposeful, and connected to measurable student outcomes.

Use of Time

Spending Time Where It Matters

The goal is for at least 80% of the school counselor's time to be devoted to direct and indirect services to students.

80%+Target time devoted to direct and indirect student services
DirectInstruction, appraisal, advisement, individual school counseling, and small-group school counseling
IndirectConsultation, collaboration, and referrals provided on behalf of students
Why this matters: Protecting school counselor time keeps the program focused on students rather than allowing duties unrelated to the school counseling program to take over the day.
Shared Leadership

Houston High School School Counseling Advisory Council

The Advisory Council provides feedback, support, and guidance so the school counseling program remains responsive to students and the wider school community.

Purpose of the Advisory Council

The council reviews program goals, student needs, school counseling activities, and program results. Members offer feedback that can strengthen services, improve access, and help connect the school counseling program to community opportunities and resources.

Who May Serve?

  • Students
  • Parents and guardians
  • Teachers
  • School administrators
  • Community representatives
  • Postsecondary partners
  • Business and career partners

Join Our Advisory Council

Your voice matters. Houston High School welcomes students, parents, educators, and community members who are interested in strengthening our Comprehensive School Counseling Program. Interested volunteers may speak with the School Counseling Office to express interest in serving.
