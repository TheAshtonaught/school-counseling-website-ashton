What It Means

What Does Define Mean?

The Define component establishes the foundation of the Houston High School Comprehensive School Counseling Program.

It identifies what our school counseling program believes about students, what we hope students will achieve, and the professional standards that guide school counseling practice. Our program is built on the belief that every student can learn, grow, develop meaningful goals, and prepare for a successful future.

The school counseling program supports the whole student by addressing academic development, career development, and social/emotional development.

Our Vision

The vision of the Houston High School School Counseling Department is that every student will graduate as a confident, capable, and prepared young adult with the academic foundation, social/emotional skills, self-awareness, and postsecondary plan necessary to successfully launch into college, career, military service, technical training, or another meaningful pathway.

Our Mission

The mission of the Houston High School School Counseling Department is to provide an equitable, comprehensive, data-informed school counseling program that supports the academic, career, and social/emotional development of every student. Through instruction, advisement, appraisal, counseling, collaboration, and advocacy, the school counseling program empowers students to recognize their strengths, overcome barriers, make informed decisions, establish meaningful goals, and develop a clear plan for life after high school.

Developmental Domains

Supporting the Whole Student

A

Academic Development

Academic development focuses on helping students acquire the attitudes, knowledge, and skills necessary to succeed in school and prepare for future educational opportunities.

The school counseling program supports academic development through goal-setting, course planning, study and organizational skills, graduation planning, academic interventions, and helping students connect current academic choices to future goals.

C

Career Development

Career development helps students understand their interests, abilities, values, and opportunities so they can make informed decisions about life after high school.

Students may explore colleges, technical programs, military pathways, apprenticeships, certifications, and careers. Our goal is for every student to graduate with a realistic and meaningful postsecondary launch plan.

S

Social/Emotional Development

Social/emotional development helps students understand themselves, manage challenges, develop healthy relationships, make responsible decisions, and advocate for themselves.

Support may address communication, coping skills, problem-solving, stress management, conflict resolution, self-confidence, belonging, decision-making, and healthy relationships.

Ethical Practice

Confidentiality & Its Limits

Ethical Obligation of Confidentiality

Students should feel safe seeking help from their school counselor. School counselors have an ethical responsibility to respect student privacy and protect confidential information to the greatest extent possible. Information shared during school counseling services is treated respectfully and is not shared simply because a student seeks support.

Limits to Confidentiality

There are circumstances in which a school counselor may need to share information to protect a student or another person or to meet legal and ethical responsibilities.

  • Risk of harm to self
  • Threat of serious harm to another person
  • Suspected abuse or neglect
  • Disclosure required by law or policy
  • Other significant safety concerns

When possible and appropriate, the school counselor will explain what information needs to be shared and why.
